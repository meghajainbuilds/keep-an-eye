# Keep an Eye

A self-hosted shopping collection: save a product while browsing, find it again, and watch for a verified price drop.

## Demo with synthetic products

1. Configure a private deployment using the example environment file.
2. Set up the iPhone Shortcut and share a product page.
3. Browse the collection and show the automatic 20% watch.
4. Demonstrate search, editable categories, and stop/resume controls.
5. Explain that readable merchant prices and phone notification permission are required. Do not claim a phone alert was delivered without observing it.

Use fabricated data in public screenshots and demonstrations. Never display private keys, personal collections, or production configuration.
